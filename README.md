For your platform, you should build two separate workflows because the process differs depending on where the death occurs.

SCENARIO 1 – Death at Home

Stage 1 – Death occurs

Family calls:

 Emergency services (112) if unexpected.

 GP (Hausarzt) if expected and available.

If the death is unexpected, the police and emergency doctor may also attend.

Stage 2 – Doctor attends

The doctor:

 Confirms death.

 Carries out an external examination.

 Determines whether the death appears:

 Natural

 Unnatural

 Unclear

The doctor issues the Todesbescheinigung (medical death certificate).

If the death is suspicious, the police and public prosecutor may become involved, and the body may be taken for a forensic examination before any funeral arrangements can continue.
